## One sentence

Forward a recall notice. NoticeProof verifies each claim against authoritative evidence, switches the consumer to a trusted contact channel, and tracks the remedy journey.

## Short description

Recall messages are not simply real or fake: a message can accurately describe a real CPSC recall while substituting an unsafe login, email, or refund destination. NoticeProof treats the inbound message as untrusted claims, acquires official evidence independently, applies deterministic authority and exact-identifier rules, and shows why each claim is supported, contradicted, narrowed, or unresolved. If a verified email action exists, the consumer reviews the exact recipient and redacted payload before AgentMail starts a new trusted thread.

## Sponsor responsibilities

- **Convex:** typed operational state, capability-scoped cases, transactions, files, schedules, idempotency, webhooks, components, append-only evidence records, and realtime UI.
- **OpenAI:** strict ClaimEnvelope extraction and bounded selection of explanation templates; it cannot set the verdict, authority, recipient, or action eligibility.
- **Firecrawl:** search, scrape, and durable crawl of current official evidence with provenance and content hashes.
- **AgentMail:** persistent deliberate-forwarding inbox, signed/deduplicated inbound events, human-approved verified-channel delivery, thread status, and replies.

## Honest limitations

The MVP covers U.S. CPSC consumer-product recalls. No authoritative match is not labeled safe. Email actions exist only when an authoritative source independently establishes the recipient. Phone, retailer, physical return, and web-form remedies remain explicit human actions. NoticeProof is not affiliated with CPSC or a manufacturer and does not provide legal advice.
